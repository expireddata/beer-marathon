The itinerary

Eight stops, roughly 5 km apart

* Opening hours unconfirmed — worth checking nearer the day.

How it works. Start at Hexham railway station and run the Tyne Valley eastward, stopping at each pub for a pint before pressing on. Most of the pubs open at midday (The Pele and The Wellington from 11:00), so the default 11:00 start brings you to the first stop just before noon. The legs get shorter in the middle and there's a cheeky climb up to Horsley, the shortest leg on paper (3.2 km) but the one that'll hurt 😅. Finish line is a well-earned pint at The Engine Inn, Walbottle. Sensible drinking, sensible shoes, and a plan for getting home afterwards!
