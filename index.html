The eight stops

The running order, roughly 5 km apart
How it went. We set off from Hexham railway station and ran the Tyne Valley eastward, stopping at each pub for a pint before pressing on. The legs got shorter in the middle, and that cheeky climb up to Horsley — the shortest leg on paper (3.2 km) — hurt exactly as much as promised 😅. The finish line was a very well-earned pint at The Engine Inn, Walbottle. Sensible shoes, questionable decisions, and every single one of us got home. 🍻
